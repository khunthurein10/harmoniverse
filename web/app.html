    Appendix

    What each field in the data and CSV files means. From the README of the harmonized export.

    S-NPP VIIRS scale

    Fields

    FieldMeaning
    latitude, longitudeCentre of the 0.25° box (about 28 km at the equator).
    year, monthCalendar month, UTC dates.
    country, country_code, provinceThe place that holds the box centre (Natural Earth admin areas). Coastal boxes take the nearest province within 0.25°.
    crosses_borderThe box also covers part of another country. It still counts fully toward the place holding its centre.
    regionGFED basis region (14 regions, GFED4.1s map).
    land_typeForest, woody savanna, savanna/grass/shrub, cropland, wetland or other (MODIS land cover, grouped as in GFED5). In box files: the type with the most hotspots that month.
    fire_type (type of burn)The kind of land GFED reports burning most in the box that month. It describes the box, not each hotspot. 2003–2022 from GFED5.1, 2023–2024 from GFED5NRT, blank from 2025.
    daynightD = afternoon pass (13:30), N = night pass (01:30), ALL = both.
    harmonized, harmonized_day, harmonized_nightHotspot count on the S-NPP VIIRS 375 m scale.
    low90, high90The 90% range. Equal to harmonized when the month was fully observed.
    uncertainty_rmseTypical error. Converted months: from the 2012–2014 test. Filled months: from the gap-filling tests. 0 when fully observed.
    aqua_raw, terra_rawOriginal MODIS counts after cleaning. Blank from 2026.
    snpp_observedReal S-NPP count. Blank before 2012.
    viirs_only_addedConverted months: fires added back where MODIS saw nothing, spread over the region’s boxes. Already included in harmonized.
    sourceHow the value was made: observed, filled with NOAA-20, gap-filled (scaled), or converted from MODIS.
    days_filledMissing or partial S-NPP days that were filled that month (0 = none).
    outage_aqua, outage_terra, outage_snppThat satellite had more than 2 days without data that month.
    above_training_rangeThe count was larger than anything the model learned from, so the lookup table was used instead.
